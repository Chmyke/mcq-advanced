# THEME revolution | The French Revolution
COURSE: History, chapter 1, the French Revolution

## Q storming-bastille | level 1 | 20s
In which year was the Bastille stormed?

- [ ] 1792
  > In 1792 the monarchy fell and the Republic was proclaimed.
- [x] 1789
  > On 14 July 1789, the people of Paris took the fortress.
- [ ] 1799
  > In 1799 Bonaparte seized power in the coup of 18 Brumaire.

RULE: Place each event of the Revolution on a timeline from 1789 to 1799.
REVIEW: History, chapter 1, timeline

## Q declaration-rights | level 2 | 30s
Which principle does the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen of August 1789 state?

- [x] Men are equal in rights
  > Its first article: men are born and remain free and equal in rights.
- [ ] Kings rule by divine right
  > That is absolute monarchy, which the Declaration replaces with the sovereignty of the nation.
- [ ] Women have the vote
  > French women only got the vote in 1944.

RULE: The 1789 Declaration grounds rights in human nature and sovereignty in the nation.
REVIEW: History, chapter 1, the 1789 Declaration

## Q order-of-events | level 3 | 40s
Which chronological order is right?

- [ ] Execution of Louis XVI, storming of the Bastille, coronation of Napoleon
  > Louis XVI was executed in January 1793, after the storming of the Bastille.
- [ ] Storming of the Bastille, coronation of Napoleon, execution of Louis XVI
  > The coronation took place in December 1804, long after the king's execution.
- [x] Storming of the Bastille, execution of Louis XVI, coronation of Napoleon
  > July 1789, January 1793, December 1804.

RULE: To put events in order, first find the year of each.
REVIEW: History, chapter 1, timeline

## Q causes-revolution | level 4 | 40s
Which of these helped to bring about the Revolution in 1789?

- [x] The financial crisis of the monarchy
  > The kingdom was in debt, which led the king to summon the Estates General.
- [ ] The defeat at Waterloo
  > Waterloo was in 1815: the end of the Empire, after the Revolution.
- [x] The spread of the ideas of the Enlightenment
  > Montesquieu, Voltaire and Rousseau criticised absolute rule and privilege.
- [ ] The rise of the railway
  > Railways spread in France from the 1830s.

RULE: A cause always comes before the event it explains.
REVIEW: History, chapter 1, the causes of the Revolution

# THEME twentieth-century | The 20th century
COURSE: History, chapter 4, wars and democracies in the 20th century

## Q trigger-1914 | level 2 | 30s
Which event set off the crisis that led to the First World War in 1914?

- [ ] The invasion of Poland
  > It opened the Second World War, in September 1939.
- [x] The Sarajevo assassination
  > On 28 June 1914, Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria was killed there.
- [ ] The Wall Street Crash
  > The crash came in October 1929, after the war.

RULE: Tell the trigger of a war apart from its deeper causes.
REVIEW: History, chapter 4, the First World War

## Q depression-1930s | level 3 | 40s
Why did the crisis of the 1930s help authoritarian regimes in Europe?

- [x] Mass unemployment discredited democracies
  > Ruined and out of work, many voters turned to those who promised order.
- [ ] It abolished the vote everywhere
  > Elections went on: in Germany, the Nazis rose precisely through the ballot box.
- [ ] It ended the Treaty of Versailles
  > Hitler challenged the treaty; the crisis itself did not cancel it.

RULE: An economic crisis turns political when it destroys trust in institutions.
REVIEW: History, chapter 4, the 1930s

## Q wall-1961 | level 4 | 40s
A photograph from 1961 shows a wall cutting a city in two. Which city and which context is it?

- [ ] Vienna, at the fall of Austria-Hungary
  > Austria-Hungary broke up in 1918, and Vienna was never cut by a wall.
- [ ] Warsaw, under Nazi occupation
  > The wall of the Warsaw ghetto dates from 1940, during the Second World War.
- [x] Berlin, during the Cold War
  > East Germany built the wall in August 1961; it fell on 9 November 1989.

RULE: Date a document before interpreting it: the date gives the context.
REVIEW: History, chapter 4, the Cold War

## Q women-vote | level 2 | 20s
True or false? In France, women voted for the first time in 1945.

- [x] True
  > They were given the vote in 1944 and used it in the local elections of April 1945.
- [ ] False
  > After the 1944 ordinance, the first election open to women did take place in 1945.

RULE: Tell the date of a right apart from the date it was first used.
REVIEW: History, chapter 4, democracy in France
