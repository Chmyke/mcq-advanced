## Q ohms-law | level 2 | 30s
A current of 0.02 A flows through a 100 Ω resistor. What is the voltage across it?

- [ ] 5000 V
  > That is $R$ divided by $I$: Ohm's law multiplies them.
- [x] 2 V
  > $V = R \times I = 100 \times 0.02 = 2$ V.
- [ ] 0.0002 V
  > That is $I$ divided by $R$: Ohm's law multiplies them.

RULE: Ohm's law: $V = R \times I$, with $V$ in volts, $R$ in ohms and $I$ in amperes.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 2, Ohm's law

## Q lamp-energy | level 3 | 40s
A 60 W lamp stays on for 2 hours. How much energy does it use?

- [x] 0.12 kWh
  > $E = P \times t = 60 \times 2 = 120$ Wh, that is 0.12 kWh.
- [ ] 120 kWh
  > The product is 120 Wh: the conversion to kilowatt-hours was forgotten.
- [ ] 30 Wh
  > That is $P$ divided by $t$: energy is a product.

RULE: $E = P \times t$; in watts and hours, the energy is in watt-hours.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 2, power and energy

## Q parallel-resistors | level 4 | 40s
Two 10 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. What is the equivalent resistance?

- [ ] 20 Ω
  > That is the equivalent resistance in series, where resistances add up.
- [ ] 10 Ω
  > In parallel, the current has two paths: the resistance goes down.
- [x] 5 Ω
  > $\frac{1}{R} = \frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$, so $R = 5$ Ω.

RULE: In parallel, $\frac{1}{R} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$; the equivalent resistance is smaller than each.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 2, combining resistors

## Q series-circuit | level 3 | 30s
In a series circuit powered by a battery, which statements are true?

- [x] The current is the same at every point
  > There is only one path: the same current flows through every component.
- [ ] The voltage is the same across every component
  > That holds in parallel; in series, the voltages are shared out.
- [x] If one lamp blows, the others go out
  > The circuit is broken: no current flows any more.
- [ ] The voltages of the components multiply
  > They add up: their sum equals the battery voltage.

RULE: In series, there is one current and the voltages add up.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 2, circuit laws

# THEME chemistry | Chemical change
COURSE: Physics and chemistry, chapter 5, chemical change

## Q sodium-symbol | level 1 | 20s
What is the chemical symbol of sodium?

- [ ] So
  > No element has this symbol.
- [x] Na
  > It comes from its Latin name, natrium.
- [ ] S
  > S is the symbol of sulfur.

RULE: A chemical symbol starts with a capital letter, followed by at most one small letter.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 5, periodic table

## Q methane-combustion | level 3 | 40s
Which equation of the complete combustion of methane is balanced?

- [x] $CH_4 + 2O_2 \to CO_2 + 2H_2O$
  > Each side has 1 C, 4 H and 4 O.
- [ ] $CH_4 + O_2 \to CO_2 + H_2O$
  > The right has 3 O and 2 H, against 2 O and 4 H on the left.
- [ ] $CH_4 + 2O_2 \to CO_2 + H_2O$
  > H and O are unbalanced: one water molecule is missing.

RULE: An equation is balanced when each element has as many atoms on both sides.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 5, reaction equations

## Q dilution-ph | level 4 | 40s
A hydrochloric acid solution of pH 2 is diluted 10 times. What is the new pH?

- [ ] 1
  > A lower pH would mean more acidic: diluting does the opposite.
- [ ] 20
  > The pH does not multiply, and it stays at or below 14 in water.
- [x] 3
  > The concentration of $H^+$ ions is divided by 10, so the pH rises by 1.

RULE: For a strong acid, diluting 10 times raises the pH by 1.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 5, pH and dilution

## Q mass-conservation | level 2 | 20s
True or false? In a chemical change, the total mass is conserved.

- [x] True
  > The atoms rearrange without appearing or disappearing: the mass stays the same.
- [ ] False
  > A gas escaping can look like a loss, but in a closed container the mass is conserved.

RULE: "Nothing is lost, nothing is created, everything is transformed", a saying attributed to Lavoisier.
REVIEW: Physics and chemistry, chapter 5, conservation of mass
